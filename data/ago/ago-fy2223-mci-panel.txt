10. AGO reviewed the tender recommendation report for the second WOG PCFA and found that 5 of the 108 tenderers should not have been appointed as they did not meet the evaluation criteria. The evaluation criteria published in the Invitation to Tender stated that tenderers were to be evaluated based on quality and price. Tenderers were required to obtain a final score of at least 50% to be appointed to the panel of vendors. AGO noted that the 5 tenderers were recommended for award and appointed to the panel of vendors even though they did not obtain a minimum final score of 50%. 11. AGO further noted that of the 5 tenderers who were wrongly appointed, purchases were made from 2 of them during the period 29 April 2022 to 27 July 2022. Four purchase orders with a total value of $0.39 million were issued to those 2 tenderers.
